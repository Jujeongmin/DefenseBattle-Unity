1. 개요

디펜스 배틀(이하 "앱")은 게임 진행을 저장하고 대전·랭킹·결제를 처리하기 위해 아래 정보를 처리합니다.
게임 서버는 Unity Gaming Services(Unity Technologies)를 이용하며, 광고·결제·로그인은 Google 서비스를 이용합니다.
이름, 전화번호, 이메일 주소처럼 사용자를 직접 식별하는 정보는 앱이 요청하지 않습니다.

3. 보관 기간

  • 게임 진행·닉네임·랭킹 정보는 계정이 있는 동안 보관하며, 삭제를 요청하면 30일 안에 지웁니다.
  • 구매 기록은 중복 지급 방지와 관련 법령(전자상거래법 등)에 따른 기간 동안 보관할 수 있습니다.
  • 대전 매칭 정보는 대전이 끝난 뒤 운영에 필요한 기간만 보관합니다.

5. 광고와 동의

  • 앱은 보상형 광고(이용자가 직접 눌러 보는 광고)만 표시하며, 전면·배너 광고는 없습니다.

  • 광고 ID 는 기기 설정에서 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다.

6. 아동의 개인정보

앱은 특정 연령을 대상으로 하지 않는 일반 이용가 게임이며, 아동으로부터 고의로 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다.

7. 계정과 데이터 삭제

  • 계정·게임 데이터 삭제는 anjshdkdl99@gmail.com 으로 요청해 주세요. 게임 안 닉네임과 마지막으로 플레이한 날짜(Google Play 게임즈로 로그인했다면 그 계정 이메일)를 함께 보내 주시면 본인 확인 뒤 30일 안에 서버의 계정, 진행, 랭킹 기록을 삭제하고 결과를 알려 드립니다.
  • 구매 기록 중 법령상 보관 의무가 있는 정보는 그 기간이 지난 뒤 삭제합니다.
  • 기기에 저장된 설정은 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워집니다.

English

1. Overview

Defense Battle (the "App") processes the information below to save your progress and to run matches, rankings and purchases.
The game server runs on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies); ads, payments and sign-in use Google services.
The App does not ask for information that identifies you directly, such as your name, phone number or email address.

2. Information processed and purpose

InformationProcessed byPurpose
Player ID (a random account ID created by the App), linked Google Play Games account IDUnity Gaming Services (Authentication)Sign-in, keeping your progress when you change devices
Nickname, game progress (gems, units, decks, research, chests, trophies, match record, quests, season pass)Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save, Cloud Code)Saving progress; currency and rewards are calculated on the server to prevent cheating
Nickname, trophies, winsUnity Gaming Services (Leaderboards)Showing rankings
Match records (matching time, opponent, result) and the board state during a matchUnity Gaming Services (Cloud Code, Relay)Real-time matchmaking, relaying and deciding results

Advertising ID, device information, approximate location (IP-based), ad interactionsGoogle AdMobServing and measuring rewarded ads
IP address, device and OS information, error diagnosticsUnity Gaming ServicesOperating the service, security, troubleshooting

Some information, such as settings (language, volume) and tutorial progress, is also stored on your device.

3. Retention

  • Game progress, nickname and ranking data are kept while your account exists and are deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Purchase records may be kept to prevent double grants and for the period required by applicable law.
  • Matchmaking data is kept only as long as needed to operate the service after a match ends.

4. Third-party services

The privacy policies of these services also apply:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Play Games Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information may be processed on these providers' servers, including outside your country.

5. Ads and consent

  • The App shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch. There are no interstitial or banner ads.
  • Users in regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK) are asked for consent to personalized ads through Google UMP.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Account and data deletion

  • To delete your account and game data, email anjshdkdl99@gmail.com with your in-game nickname and the date you last played (and, if you signed in with Google Play Games, that account's email). After confirming the request, we delete your account, progress and ranking records from the server within 30 days and let you know.
  • Purchase records that we must keep by law are deleted after that period ends.
  • Settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the App.
